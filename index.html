Origin

Born from the first lie humanity ever told itself, Noxveil drifts at the seam where falsehood hardens into myth. He watches with ember-eyes; he catalogues the flimsy courage of mankind like a naturalist catalogues fragile insects.

"Humans hate me because they see themselves in my eyes."
